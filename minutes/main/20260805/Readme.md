### Presentation from Robert Warren

rw: From Canada. Also work for a supply chain and tracing company.
... As part of my work, I deploy sensors. Temperature, humidity, weather.
... people typically source their sensors and provide information about the data but typically no information on quality, precision or performance.
... you also need to manage their lifecycle
... we want to meet in TPAC but the agenda is getting full too. How should we work together?

ka: Thank you for the introduction. As a co-chair of Smart Cities IG. What is your point on this?
... also what topic should we discuss in TPAC? Also, who else should we include from the group?

rw: The data is abundant but the semantics is the lacking part now.

ka: I can send a separate email about the Smart Cities IG, no problem. I was just wondering about your interest in addition.

rw: I am happy to help but it is not something I have worked with so far.
... rob smith might be of help as well
... You asked about chairs. They are aware but they are both very busy for this year.

de: I was wondering about this as it is of high interest to me in retail domain. Erich from Microsoft was looking into Digital Twins, together with Siemens.

rw: a sensor can break etc. and a digital twin actually helps in these scenarios.

mk: I would be interested in your SOSA and SSN usage. but how do you bridge to WoT aspect, which is about how you talk to the device? I would be interested to learn more.

rw: SOSA is good about describing the sensor, its accuracy etc. But it lacks deployment aspects, which is the focus of WOT.

ek: I want to ask about which specs do you want to contribute to?

rw: I got recommended WoT 3 weeks ago and I have only looked into use cases document. So it is in the beginning for me.

sk: I had a similar question. Please look at the TD where you can find a semantic description of the device. Ege and Michael are leading this TF so you can talk to them.
